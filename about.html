ABOUT US

A History of
Academic Excellence

Founded in 1965 by Dr. Eleanor Greenfield, Greenfield Academy has grown from a small neighborhood school into one of the region's premier educational institutions. For nearly six decades, we have been committed to developing well-rounded individuals who excel academically, grow morally, and contribute meaningfully to society.

"Education is not preparation for life; education is life itself. At Greenfield, we believe every child carries a spark of genius — our mission is to ignite it."

Dr. Margaret Osei-Bonsu Principal, Greenfield Academy

Our Vision

To be a beacon of holistic education, producing graduates who lead with integrity and innovate for the common good.

Our Mission

Deliver innovative, inclusive education that equips every student for academic achievement and global citizenship.
